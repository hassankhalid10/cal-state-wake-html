Your Premier Southern California Inland Oasis

Elevating Every Moment on the Water

Welcome to Cal State Wake, a family-owned and operated full-service boat dealership located in Castaic, California. As Southern California's premier dealer for Centurion® and Supreme boats, we are dedicated to providing a unique boating experience for families and boat enthusiasts throughout Los Angeles County and beyond. Serving not only our local community but also the surrounding areas of Southern California, we strive to elevate your boating adventures with our selection of high-quality new and used boats and professional maintenance services.

Here at Cal State Wake, we understand that quality family time is about creating exceptional experiences. We believe that the joy of boating should encompass not just exhilaration and excitement, but also education, support, and dependability. When you incorporate recreational watersports into your family time, you create cherished memories and strengthen family bonds.
